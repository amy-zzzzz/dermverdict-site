DermVerdict

What do dermatologists think about your skincare?

DermVerdict is an iPhone app that shows what dermatologists have said about skincare products in their public YouTube videos. Search a product or scan its barcode, and see each dermatologist's take, with a link to the moment in the video where they said it.
